Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how hun4000 ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application, Tap Ascent (the "App").

1. Our Data Collection Stance

We do not directly collect or store any personal information (such as real names, email addresses, or physical locations) from our users. We believe in minimal data usage. However, to provide core functionalities like advertisements and leaderboards, our App integrates specific third-party services that process limited technical data automatically.

2. Third-Party Services & Data Processing

The following services are used strictly for app functionality. Their data practices are governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: Used for serving advertisements. AdMob automatically processes technical data such as Device Advertising ID, IP address, and approximate location to deliver relevant ads. We do not have access to this data; it is processed solely by Google.
    Google Advertising Privacy Policy
  • SilentWolf: Used for the global leaderboard feature. This service stores user-defined nicknames (random or custom) and high scores. No real-world identity, email, or device ID is shared with SilentWolf by us. The data is used exclusively to display the ranking list within the game.
    SilentWolf Privacy Policy (or applicable terms)
  • Google Firebase: Used for crash reporting and stability. It collects anonymous crash logs and device model information to help us fix technical errors. No personal user content is tracked.
    Firebase Privacy Policy

3. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect personal data directly, and our third-party services (AdMob) are configured to limit data collection, we prioritize the safety of younger users.

4. User Rights

Since we do not store your personal data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to delete. For data collected by third parties (AdMob, Firebase), users can manage their privacy settings directly through their device settings (e.g., resetting Advertising ID) or by contacting the respective service providers.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page and are effective immediately.
